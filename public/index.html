Where do I find my link?

On TU Dublin's timetable site

Open your personal timetable and look for Subscribe or Add to calendar, then copy the link it gives you. It usually ends in .ics or starts with webcal://.

Already in your iPhone calendar?

Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Subscribed Calendars → tap your timetable → copy the address.

Google Calendar

On a computer: Settings → choose the timetable → Secret address in iCal format.

Privacy Policy Terms of Use

Studyslot is independent and not connected to any college. Always check your college's official timetable.

Studyslot keeps your link and choices only on this device. Each refresh fetches your timetable fresh, and it's never saved on our server.

Which groups are you in?

Some classes are listed for several groups at the same time. Pick yours so you only see your own. You can change this in Settings.

Share with a friend

Your friend scans this with their phone camera, or you send them the link. They'll see your timetable in Studyslot so you can find free time together. The link is private: it doesn't show your timetable link, but anyone who has it can see your classes, so only share it with people you trust. You can reset it at any time.

Stop sharing

Shared it with someone you'd rather didn't see your timetable? Reset your link: every link and QR code you've shared stops working. Then send the new one to the friends you still want. Your phone's calendar isn't affected.

Add a friend

Paste the Studyslot link your friend sent you.

Add friend?

Using Studyslot from your Home Screen? Copy the link, open Studyslot, then Friends → Add a friend → paste.
