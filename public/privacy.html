Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no cookies.
  • Your timetable link and settings are saved only on your own device.
  • When the app needs your timetable, our server fetches it from your link, tidies it up, sends it back to you and doesn't keep a copy.
  • We never sell or share your information for marketing.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Remove my timetable from this device.

1. Who we are

Studyslot is a free, independent web app run by [Your name], an individual based in Ireland ("we", "us"). For the limited personal data described below, we are the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Irish Data Protection Acts 1988–2018.

Contact: hello@studyslot.ie

2. What information is involved, and why

On your device

Studyslot saves the following in your browser's storage on your phone or computer. We cannot see it.

  • Your timetable calendar link, and a copy of your timetable so the app works offline.
  • Your college, course and year, the lab groups you chose, and modules you've hidden.
  • The name you give for friends, and the Studyslot links of any friends you add.

To delete it, use Settings → Remove my timetable from this device, remove friends in Settings, or clear this site's data in your browser.

When Studyslot reads your timetable

Your device sends your timetable link to our server. The server downloads the calendar from that address, tidies the class names and rooms, and sends the result back to your device. Your link and timetable are not saved in any database, file or log by Studyslot. Your college's server sees the request coming from our hosting provider, not from you.

If Studyslot runs on its own web address, our hosting provider may keep a copy of the college's calendar file for up to 15 minutes, so that many refreshes in a row don't each reach your college's server. It's then discarded.

"Add to your calendar"

This creates a calendar link that contains your timetable link and your choices, encoded but not encrypted. Your calendar app (for example Apple, Google or Microsoft) stores that link and requests it regularly to stay up to date. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do. To stop it, remove the calendar subscription in your calendar app.

Friends

Your friend link contains your timetable link, your choices and the name you chose. Anyone you send it to can see your timetable in Studyslot, and it's saved on their device. Only share it with people you trust. We can't recall a link once it's shared. If you need to stop someone seeing your timetable, ask your college whether they can issue you a new calendar link.

Friends' links you add are saved only on your device and are used only to show your shared free time.

Hosting and security

Studyslot is hosted by Cloudflare, which acts as our processor under its Data Processing Addendum. To deliver the app and protect it from abuse (for example, our limit on how often one visitor can look up timetables), Cloudflare processes technical details of each request, such as your IP address, browser type, the time and the web address requested. Cloudflare operates worldwide; transfers outside the European Economic Area are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Studyslot loads no third-party fonts, scripts, analytics or advertising. Everything it needs comes from Studyslot itself.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply and to fix problems, and delete them within 12 months. Feedback sent from the app includes your college, course, year, app version and device type, but never your timetable link.

4. Cookies and device storage

Studyslot uses no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage only for the features you ask for (remembering your timetable and settings). This is strictly necessary for the service, so it doesn't need a consent banner under the ePrivacy Regulations (S.I. No. 336 of 2011). We don't use analytics. If we ever add simple visitor counts, they will be cookie-free and we'll update this policy first.

5. How long information is kept

  • On your device: until you remove it.
  • On our servers: not kept. Timetables are processed in memory and discarded.
  • Hosting logs: kept by Cloudflare for short periods under its own policy.
  • Emails: deleted within 12 months.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Because we don't keep your timetable, you can exercise most of these yourself in the app. For anything else, email hello@studyslot.ie and we'll reply within one month.

You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission: www.dataprotection.ie, 21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28, Ireland.

7. Age

Studyslot is for students aged 16 and over. If you're under 16, please only use it with the agreement of a parent or guardian.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If a change affects you significantly, we'll tell you in the app.
